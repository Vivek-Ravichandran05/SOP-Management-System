Title: Employee Onboarding

Category: HR

Purpose: To ensure all new employees are onboarded efficiently and consistently

Scope: All departments, all new employees

Responsibilities:
	1.HR: Collect documents, create profiles
	2.IT: Set up accounts and devices
	3.Procedure / Steps:
		A.HR sends welcome email
		B.Collect personal and professional documents
		C.IT creates system accounts
		D.Assign a mentor
